🇨🇲 Our Story

Built for Cameroon.
By people who get it.

MaisonQuest was created to solve a real problem that affects people all across Cameroon — finding a home shouldn't mean scrolling through blurry WhatsApp photos, dodging scams, or missing out because you're searching from abroad.

The Origin

The way people find homes in Cameroon needed to change

For too long, finding a property in Cameroon meant relying on WhatsApp groups flooded with reposts, Facebook pages with no filter, and word-of-mouth chains that left renters — especially those searching from abroad — completely in the dark.

MaisonQuest was born in 2026 from a simple question: what if there was one place where you could search across all of Cameroon, see verified listings with real photos and real prices, and contact the agency directly — without the noise?

We built MaisonQuest to give agencies a professional reach beyond social media, and to give renters — whether they're in Cameroon or searching from Europe, North America, or anywhere in the diaspora — a search experience that actually works.

What We Do

A platform built on three simple promises

Our Journey

How we got here

Early 2026

The problem becomes impossible to ignore

Finding property in Cameroon was stuck in the past — scattered across WhatsApp groups and Facebook pages with no structure, no verification, and no real reach. People abroad trying to secure housing back home had it even worse. MaisonQuest starts as an idea to fix this.

2026

Building the platform

The focus: create a single, professional destination where verified agencies can list properties with real photos, accurate prices, and full details — replacing the noise of social media with something renters can actually rely on.

Now

Live and growing

MaisonQuest is live — bilingual, nationwide, and built for both renters on the ground in Cameroon and those searching from the diaspora. We're onboarding agencies, adding listings, and expanding city by city.

Next

Every city in Cameroon

From Douala and Yaoundé to Buea, Bamenda, Bafoussam, Limbe and beyond — our goal is to make MaisonQuest the default way people find property anywhere in Cameroon.
